How It Works

The Laser Cleaning Process

01
Surface Assessment
Our technician inspects the surface, identifies contamination type and depth, and selects optimal laser parameters for the material.
02
Laser Application
Concentrated short laser pulses are directed precisely at the contaminated layer. No masking, no solvents — only light.
03
Vaporisation
Microexplosions at the surface instantly vaporise contaminants layer by layer. The substrate beneath remains completely untouched.
04
Extraction & Result
Vaporised particles are captured by an integrated extraction system. The result: a pore-deep clean surface with zero residue or secondary waste.
